# Chuck McMahon

**Founder of Asterion | Building CROS, the Customer Revenue Operating System**

I've spent more than 24 years building and leading customer-facing organizations in B2B SaaS. My work spans Customer Success, Customer Experience, Revenue Operations, retention, expansion, and the economics of serving customers.

Across those environments, I kept encountering the same problem: companies have more information about their customers, but the decisions and actions that produce customer revenue still depend heavily on individual judgment and follow-through. I'm building Asterion to make that execution more systematic, consistent, and scalable.

## What I'm building

**CROS** is a Customer Revenue Operating System for B2B SaaS. It is designed to turn customer and business signals into prioritized decisions and coordinated action, then learn from the results to improve retention, expansion, and cost-to-serve.

CROS is built on **Adaptive Execution**, Asterion's architecture for determining what matters, deciding what should happen next, coordinating the appropriate people and technology, and improving through learning.

## My role in the build

I lead product direction, customer discovery, operating logic, prioritization, and acceptance of the product's behavior. I use AI-assisted development to translate that domain knowledge into working software, with GitHub supporting the development process.

My current focus is the CROS MVP and a Deployment Portal that helps coordinate implementation, information requests, responsibilities, and decisions. The build is guided by practical validation: useful signals, sound recommendations, leader visibility, improved outcomes, and a learning loop.

## How I approach the work

- Start with the customer and business outcome.
- Understand the decisions and constraints behind that outcome.
- Build the smallest useful capability, test it, and learn.
- Use AI to increase execution capacity while retaining founder accountability for product decisions.

I'm interested in conversations with B2B SaaS leaders, potential design partners, and people building practical AI-enabled operating systems.
